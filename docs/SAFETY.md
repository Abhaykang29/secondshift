# Safety

## Honest limits
SecondShift is a screening and awareness tool. It is not a medical device, not an emergency alarm, not a replacement for an occupational therapist or a professional babyproofer, and it cannot guarantee anything is safe.

## Disclaimers
The footer disclaimer (Appendix C) appears on every page: "SecondShift is a screening tool, not a medical device or an emergency alarm. It can miss things. For a full assessment, ask an occupational therapist or a certified professional."

## Refusal policy
- Rules that ask a camera to see the unseeable (future, intentions, internal states, smells, temperature, identity, audio) are refused with observable alternatives (spec section 9.12).
- Watch in bathrooms is always refused.
- Any feature request that drifts toward tracking people gets refused here/in DECISIONS.md (spec section 14.10).
- Medical questions in Ask are answered with a gentle suggestion to ask their doctor, never with medical advice.
